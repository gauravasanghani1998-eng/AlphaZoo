# Privacy Policy for AlphaZoo

**Last updated: February 3, 2026**

## Information We Collect

AlphaZoo is an educational app designed for children to learn the alphabet. We are committed to protecting your privacy and only collect minimal information necessary for the app's functionality.

### Information Collected Automatically
- **Device Information**: Basic device information (OS version, device type) for app performance
- **Usage Analytics**: Anonymous usage statistics to improve the app (optional)

### Information We Do NOT Collect
- Personal information
- Location data
- Contact information
- Payment information
- Any data from children under 13

## How We Use Information

The limited information we collect is used solely to:
- Ensure the app functions properly on your device
- Improve app performance and user experience
- Fix technical issues

## Data Sharing

We do NOT share, sell, or rent any collected information to third parties.

## Children's Privacy

AlphaZoo is designed for children aged 3-7. We comply with COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) and do not collect personal information from children.

## Third-Party Services
